Hair is hygiene-sensitive, so nobody in this category takes it back once it's been worn. That's normal and expected. But your StyleSeat bio says “No Refunds” for services, and stacking a hard product policy on top of that is where people get annoyed enough to say so publicly.

register #12 · blocks: two required policy pages
