Buying here, installing somewhere else

Most of our orders today go to people Skye installs for herself. If you're taking the hair to your own stylist, we'd rather be straight with you about what we can and can't tell them yet.

What we can confirm

  • Exact length and texture of what you ordered
  • The price you paid at that length — no hidden tiers
  • Two bundles covers bob through natural length; her pricing shifts past 3 bundles
  • 5x5 HD lace on the closures
Hand your stylist the length, the texture, and the bundle count. That's the part we're certain about.

What we can't tell you yet — and won't guess

  • Hair type and origin
  • Whether it's been processed, and how
  • Whether it takes colour, and how it behaves if it does
  • How long it lasts with normal wear
If your stylist needs those answers before they'll work with it, wait. We're confirming them with our supplier and they'll be on every product page before launch. We'd rather lose the order than have you hand a stylist a spec we made up.
Colour work: don't let anyone colour it the day of your install.

Skye's own rule is that hair gets dropped off at least four days before a colour service. If your stylist is colouring it, give them the same runway.

Why people still drive to Lafayette

Some of Skye's clients travel hours because the hair and the hands come from the same person — she tells you how much to order, then installs what she recommended. If that appeals more than coordinating two strangers, it's worth a look.
