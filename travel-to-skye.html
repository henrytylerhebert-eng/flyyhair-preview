Coming to see her

Planning a trip around an appointment

People already drive hours for this — one client's review mentions an hour each way being worth it. If you're coming from further, here's the honest state of things.

What's fixed

  • Tuesday to Friday, 9am–4pm. Closed Saturday, Sunday and Monday.
  • Monday appointments can be opened for a +$100 fee
  • 7am and 7pm slots can be opened for a +$60 fee
  • A full sew-in runs 4 hours; a removal and re-install runs 5
  • One client at a time, in a private suite in Lafayette
A four-to-five hour appointment inside a Tuesday–Friday 9–4 week means very few slots. Plan early rather than close.

What isn't sorted yet — read this before booking flights

  • No deposit is taken on any service today. Nothing holds your slot but the booking.
  • Cancellation terms are 24 hours: 25% inside that window, 50% for a no-show
  • Your hair still needs to arrive before you do — 5–7 business days shipping
We're not taking travel bookings as a product yet, and this is why. Asking someone to buy a plane ticket around an appointment that carries no deposit isn't fair to either side. Deposits are the next thing to fix.
If you're coming anyway

Order your hair first, message her with your dates before you book anything non-refundable, and build in a buffer day. Her cancellation terms and hours are on StyleSeat.
